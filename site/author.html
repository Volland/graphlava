Author

Volodymyr Pavlyshyn

Principal engineer and architect in Berlin. I work on memory for agents and humans: knowledge graphs, retrieval, temporal and verifiable data, and the identity and trust between agents.

Why graphlava

Agents need to say things about what they know: how sure they are, where a fact came from, which context it holds in. Statement graphs give every fact an ID so that it can carry those things. graphlava is the embedded, current-state side of that idea; its sibling tiramemsu is the layered side that never forgets.
